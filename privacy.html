AI Video Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Overview

AI Video Studio is a locally operated application for creating, reviewing, and preparing short-form videos. Integration with social media publishing services is under development.

2. Information We May Process

When social media integrations are enabled, the application may process account identifiers, authorized access tokens, publishing permissions, video files, captions, publishing status, and related information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized connections, prepare content, submit publishing requests, and report the results of those requests. Information is not sold to advertisers.

4. Local Storage

The application currently stores generated videos, scripts, configuration files, and processing records on the operator's computer. Social media token storage and publishing integrations have not yet been implemented. This policy will be updated before those features are activated.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may communicate with third-party services for video generation, media retrieval, and authorized publishing. Information shared with those services is subject to their applicable privacy policies.

6. Security

The operator is responsible for securing the computer, API credentials, and files used by the application. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Access, Revocation, and Deletion

Users may revoke social media authorization through the relevant platform's account settings. Requests to access or delete application-held information can be submitted through the contact address listed below.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as new features are added. The updated version will be published on this page.
